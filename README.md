# MEDX

## Modern Electronic Digital eXperience for Healthcare

A full-stack hospital management platform built to streamline patient care, appointment scheduling, and medical record management. MEDX is designed to demonstrate software engineering, system design, and Technical Program Management practices through the complete software development lifecycle.

---

## Project Overview

Hospitals often rely on disconnected systems and manual processes that slow down patient care and increase administrative work. MEDX aims to provide a centralized platform where healthcare professionals can securely manage patients, appointments, and medical records.


---

## Project Goals

- Improve patient registration and record management
- Simplify appointment scheduling
- Reduce administrative workload
- Improve communication between hospital staff
- Demonstrate modern software engineering practices
